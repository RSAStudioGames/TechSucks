## Sites List

Here are some good sites for finding media. They are safer than most, but nothing is ever 100% safe. Use your own judgement.
DDL = Direct Download Link

Site: **(Torrent)** [1337x](https://1337x.to) - Has just about everything. General public torrent site. Has anime, movies, shows, music, games, software, books, etc.

### Movies / Shows

### Music

I currently don't have any [sites](https://techsucks.org/images/NoPrivateTrackers.webp) I could recommend for downloading music. I personally just use Deemix or Jdownloader to download songs off of youtube. {{< linebreak >}}
[YT-DLP](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) - (CLI tool for downloading youtube videos and playlists. {{< linebreak >}}
[Deemix](https://github.com/bambanah/deemix) - Easy to use tool for downloading songs off of deezer. You can even use a spotify API key to download playlists you've made in spotify (because who would actually use deezer lol). You will need a free deezer account, then you can go and copy your ARL token from your cookies and add that to deemix. The [EditThisCookie](https://www.editthiscookie.com/) extension for Firefox or Chromium browsers makes getting the ARL super simple. {{< linebreak >}}

## Tools

[JDownloader2](https://jdownloader.org/) - My personal favorite download manager for downloading just about anything from just about any site, including YT videos. Has a built-in account manager so you can use your premium accounts. {{< linebreak >}}
[SpotX](https://github.com/SpotX-Official/SpotX) - Ad blocker for spotify desktop (not the microsoft store version) {{< linebreak >}}
