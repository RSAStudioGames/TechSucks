Alright, I've finally gotten around to writing some more.
I've finally switched away from Windows and I'm so happy I did. CachyOS (using KDE) has been such a great experience. It's stable, fast, privacy-respecting, and fast. Did I say fast twice? Well I'll say it one more time. It's fast. Everything feels so darn snappy. Opening the application launcher (start menu equivalent on Windows) is instant, the search is both accurate and reactive, apps open incredibly fast. I just love it.

Now I'm gonna make a controversial statement. I actually like KDE. Sue me. I'm so used to the windows manager from Windows and KDE not only replicates the behaviours I'm used to, but significantly improves on them and it's overall usability.
Tempted to try out Hyprland/Niri eventually, their designs look so cool and feels quite unique.

Installation was a breeze, super simple, and the installer looked cool too. I wasn't asked to sign in to something, no endless privacy toggles to change (because it doesn't track you in the first place), and I don't have to worry about any garbage being installed.

After signing in, all my hardware just works, drivers are already there. I can play games right out of the box without needing to do any tweaks. I've found even less driver errors on CachyOS than I have on Windows even.
Wifi, bluetooth, networking, etc works nicely and is easier to work with than Windows.
While most updates still require a restart after, I'm not constantly hounded about it. Updates don't install random garbage onto my computer. My settings stay the same after updating as well (I'm looking at you both, MacOS & Windows...).

My only gripe so far in my 6 months or so of using Cachy is just a few specific Windows apps not working all too well when run through Wine/Proton.

Overall, I'm loving the OS. I have fun just messing around with all the different customization options available. The switch to Linux has been great, never imagined the experience would be this great.
