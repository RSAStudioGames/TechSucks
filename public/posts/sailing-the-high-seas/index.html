Sailing The High Seas - Piracy and You#

Yarrr! So it’s the pirate’s life for you, eh? Here’s information I’ve gleaned over the years…
Biggest thing is… If you like the software and you plan on using it a lot, purchase it to support the developers. (Unless it’s Adobe or Microsoft products)
This guide is purely for educational purposes. I will not provide tutorials on how to pirate specific software. Use your brain.

Sites List#

Here are some good sites for finding media. They are safer than most, but nothing is ever 100% safe. Use your own judgement. DDL = Direct Download Link

Site: (Torrent) 1337x - Has just about everything. General public torrent site. Has anime, movies, shows, music, games, software, books, etc.

Movies / Shows#

Site: (Torrent) 1337x (Best site IMO for movies/software/games)

Anime / Manga#

Site: (Torrent) Nyaa (Best site IMO for Anime & Manga)

Music#

I currently don’t have any sites I could recommend for downloading music. I personally just use Deemix or Jdownloader to download songs off of youtube.
YT-DLP - (CLI tool for downloading youtube videos and playlists.
Deemix - Easy to use tool for downloading songs off of deezer. You can even use a spotify API key to download playlists you’ve made in spotify (because who would actually use deezer lol). You will need a free deezer account, then you can go and copy your ARL token from your cookies and add that to deemix. The EditThisCookie extension for Firefox or Chromium browsers makes getting the ARL super simple.

Tools#

JDownloader2 - My personal favorite download manager for downloading just about anything from just about any site, including YT videos. Has a built-in account manager so you can use your premium accounts.
SpotX - Ad blocker for spotify desktop (not the microsoft store version)
MediCat - USB toolkit with everything from password removal, ransomware and malware recovery, partitioning utilities, backup and data recovery, and much more.
