The “I have nothing to hide" argument annoys me so much because it shows that tech companies and the government have done such a good at convincing people that you only need privacy if you’re doing something malicious.

If you want to start getting a better handle of your digital privacy, I recommend by first debloating your Windows 10 or 11 version by following my guide on /debloating-windows-os. This will block a lot of tracking telemetry microsoft includes which tracks nearly everything you do from searches in your start menu, opening steam, what games you play, what settings you open, etc. I’ll get a more detailed list of exactly what data microsoft collects on individuals and why people should care.
