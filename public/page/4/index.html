I’m RSA, and I’ve got ~20 years of self-taught tech experience at this point; everything from server administration and development to consulting and game design. I’m really passionate about computers and technology, and even more passionate when I’m complaining about it all.

This is info repo for all my tech/IT knowledge and whatever other projects I feel like sharing. I made this site because I really feel like the internet is going downhill fast, especially since LLMs like Ch*tGPT became a thing. It genuinely saddens me to see so little content diversity online as people’s unique creative outlets get killed off and and corporations take their places. Online freedom of speech keeps diminishing and companies restrict what you can say so they can be advertiser-friendly, all under the guise of protecting people’s feelings…

Most people only visit a handful of select websites anymore. The days of expressive and creative personal blogs are basically dead. Sure, there’s still some cool sites out there, hard to find as they may be, but they’re completely eclipsed by the corpo pages, you know the ones-- youtube, tiktok, twitter, and facebook’s AI-plagued corpse. Even as early as 2010, Wired was calling out the issue.

I’ve got two main goals for this page, share my knowledge and passion for technology to help people learn about computers and tech in a world where those skills are becoming increasingly important, while also getting back at those who profit from gatekeeping this kind of knowledge.

Throughout my tech journey, I’ve discovered many interesting creators with some great insight, namely people like Louis Rossmann, Leadius USA, Linus Tech Tips, and Level1Techs. You’re bound to see some of their influence and inspiration on here.

I’m putting most of my effort into SERVERMAXXING right now which covers a lot about AI-related hardware currently, but will be expanded to cover most general server stuff, including hardware guides and basics of what to look for depending on your use case. Once I’m finished with that page, I’ll start focusing on the other pages and expanding more.

My personal notes for installing GPU drivers. AMD drivers were a lot simpler to install, but Nvidia drivers were their own kind of problem and docs didn’t always give reproducible results. It’s probably a me problem, but this saved me lots of problems with Nvidia drivers.

GPU Drivers

Prerequisites for installing on Virtual Machines (ESXI):

pciPassthru.use64bitMMIO = TRUE

pciPassthru.64bitMMIOSizeGB = 48

(Rule of thumb: MMIO size must be a power of 2 and at least twice the value of the current total VRAM for the VM. EX: V100 16gb = 32. M40 24GB = 48.)

Useful Packages

Homebrew - https://brew.sh/

INSTALL COMMAND: /bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Homebrew/install/HEAD/install.sh)"

Homebrew is a free and open-source package manager that simplifies the installation of software on macOS and Linux.

UV - https://docs.astral.sh/uv/#installation

INSTALL COMMAND: curl -LsSf https://astral.sh/uv/install.sh | sh

UV is an extremely fast Python package and project manager written in Rust, designed to replace tools like pip and pip-tools.
